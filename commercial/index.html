Safe Installation

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and expensive water damage when a tank fails. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms.

Rapid Response

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Palo Alto Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Palo Alto and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: our technicians handle all of it on commercial equipment. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting hot water back fast, and in a multi-heater system we isolate the failed unit so the others carry the load. Once it's running, we put maintenance in place to prevent a repeat, and each response is fully documented for your records.

Preventive Care

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Palo Alto Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve testing, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.

Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and failed inspections. Businesses that commit to a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower fuel bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan sized to your property and budget.
